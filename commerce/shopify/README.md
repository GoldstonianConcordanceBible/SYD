# CANONICAL DESCRIPTION

SYD Financial Markets & Technology is a modular financial-markets and financial-technology curriculum developed by SydTek Scholars for learners, educators, and future institutional licensing.

The curriculum progresses from equities, indices, commodities, foreign exchange, and private markets into market structure, fintech, AI, blockchain infrastructure, and digital assets.
