# PLATFORM ROLES

## PURPOSE

Each platform has one primary job.

Do not force every platform to do everything.

---

# YOUTUBE

## ROLE

MEDIA REPOSITORY

Use YouTube for:

- long-form lectures,
- historical content,
- explainers,
- demonstrations,
- shorts,
- playlists,
- public discovery.

---

# SHOPIFY

## ROLE

DIRECT-TO-LEARNER COURSE HUB + COMMERCE

Use Shopify for:

- course storefront,
- enrollment / purchase path,
- lesson presentation,
- video embeds,
- downloads,
- assignments,
- course navigation,
- links to supporting resources.

---

# GITHUB

## ROLE

CURRICULUM + PROVENANCE SYSTEM

Use GitHub for:

- course definitions,
- learning outcomes,
- assessments,
- rubrics,
- media mapping,
- case studies,
- version control,
- evidence,
- revision history.

---

# SUBSTACK

## ROLE

READING + WRITING + DISCUSSION LAYER

Potential use:

- articles,
- reflections,
- assignments,
- research notes,
- long-form commentary.

---

# GDEX

## ROLE

APPLIED MARKET LABORATORY

GDex is not the curriculum.

GDex may provide an environment in which selected concepts are demonstrated.

---

# TELEGRAM

## ROLE

SEPARATE LIVE-MARKET / COMMUNITY LAYER

Telegram is not required for normal academic completion.

---

# FUTURE UNIVERSITY LMS

## ROLE

INSTITUTIONAL DELIVERY

A university may eventually deliver licensed SYD material through its own LMS.
