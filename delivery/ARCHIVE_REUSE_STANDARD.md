# SYD ARCHIVE REUSE STANDARD

## PURPOSE

SYD may contain years of educational media produced before the current curriculum architecture existed.

The archive is an asset.

---

# DO NOT ORGANIZE BY OLD BRAND ONLY

The original playlist might say:

DES-101

WEB3-201

SCI-105

or another legacy identifier.

The new curriculum asks:

WHAT DOES THIS VIDEO ACTUALLY TEACH?
