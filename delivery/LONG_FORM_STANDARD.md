# SYD LONG-FORM MEDIA STANDARD

## PURPOSE

Long-form media provides depth.

Existing long-form instruction from earlier years should be reused wherever academically appropriate.

---

# SOURCE YEARS

Potential source library:

2021

2022

2023

2024

2025

2026

and later.

---

# BEFORE REUSE

Review:

1. Is the concept still accurate?
2. Has the platform changed?
3. Has the law or regulation changed?
4. Have product mechanics changed?
5. Are screenshots outdated?
6. Does the old branding create confusion?
7. Is a new introduction sufficient?

---

# CLASSIFICATION

## EVERGREEN

Use directly.

## EVERGREEN + NEW INTRO

Content remains correct but old branding needs context.

## HISTORICAL CASE

Use specifically because it documents an earlier period.

## UPDATE REQUIRED

Concept remains useful but important factual details changed.

## RETIRE

Do not use instructionally.

Preserve for provenance where appropriate.

---

# DO NOT RE-RECORD BY DEFAULT

A 30-minute useful lecture does not need to be rerecorded simply because the title card is old.

Instead:

NEW 60–120 SECOND INTRO

↓

LEGACY LONG-FORM LECTURE

↓

NEW ASSESSMENT

That creates a current course from an existing asset.

---

# UNIVERSITY RULE

A legacy video used in a university-facing course should be contextualized.

Example:

"This lecture was originally recorded in 2022. The underlying risk-management concepts remain part of this course. Platform-specific references are historical unless otherwise noted."
