---

# DEFAULT MEDIA STRATEGY

## LONG FORM

Keep the master video on YouTube where practical.

Embed or link the video inside Shopify.

This preserves:

- one video master,
- existing YouTube analytics,
- existing comments,
- original publication history,
- playlist relationships.

---

# DIRECT VIDEO UPLOAD

Direct Shopify-hosted media may be appropriate for:

- short introductions,
- trailers,
- small instructional videos,
- product/course previews.

Do not duplicate the entire YouTube archive without a specific reason.

# VIDEO VISIBILITY

A course video may be:

PUBLIC

or

UNLISTED

where the selected hosting/delivery method permits external embedding.

Private video settings that prevent external access are not suitable for normal embedded delivery.

---

# SHORTS

Where a video host uses a separate Shorts URL format, preserve the canonical standard video URL where required by the Shopify media integration.

---

# FILE ATTACHMENTS

Shopify may also deliver supporting resources such as:

- worksheets,
- PDFs,
- templates,
- checklists,
- course resources.

Do not place confidential learner records in public storefront files.

---

# ACCESS CONTROL

Commercial access control may be handled through:

- Shopify customer accounts,
- approved access apps,
- course/LMS integration,
- other documented methods.

The academic curriculum itself should remain independent of the specific gating system.

---

# UNIVERSITY FIREWALL

Shopify is the initial direct-consumer delivery layer.

It is NOT the permanent definition of the academic course.

A university license should be portable to another LMS.
