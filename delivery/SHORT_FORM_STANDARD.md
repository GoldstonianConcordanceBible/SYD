# SYD SHORT-FORM STANDARD

## PURPOSE

Short-form media performs three jobs.

---

# JOB 1 — ORIENTATION

Explain:

Why is this lesson here?

Target:

30–90 seconds.

---

# JOB 2 — BRIDGE

Connect legacy media to the current curriculum.

Example:

"This 2021 lecture was originally recorded in a digital-markets course. Today it appears inside Financial Markets & Technology because the risk principle applies across stocks, commodities, FX, and digital assets."

---

# JOB 3 — REINFORCEMENT

One doctrine.

One concept.

One example.

Examples:

THE SYMBOL DOES NOT DEFINE THE INSTRUMENT.

A THESIS WITHOUT INVALIDATION IS INCOMPLETE.

KNOW THE EXIT BEFORE YOU ENTER.

MARKETS FIRST. TECHNOLOGY NEXT. CRYPTO LAST.

---

# SHORTS DO NOT REPLACE ASSESSMENT

A learner may watch a 45-second explanation.

Mastery still requires evidence.

# NEW RECORDING PRIORITY

Record new short-form media when:

- old branding needs explanation,
- terminology changed,
- course sequence changed,
- the platform changed,
- a concept needs a modern bridge.

Do not rerecord hours of correct material unnecessarily.
