# SYD MEDIA ASSET STANDARD

Every useful media asset should eventually receive a Media ID.

---

# FORMAT

SYD-MEDIA-[NUMBER]

Example:

SYD-MEDIA-0001

---

# ORIGINAL DATE MATTERS

Do not hide the fact that some content originated in:

2021

2022

2023

2024

2025

2026

Older material may still be academically useful.

The original date is part of the provenance.

---

# EVERGREEN CONTENT

Examples:

- risk principles,
- market psychology,
- thesis construction,
- blockchain fundamentals,
- basic financial concepts.

These may remain usable with little or no change.

---

# TIME-SENSITIVE CONTENT

Examples:

- platform interface,
- fees,
- product availability,
- regulation,
- market prices,
- current executives,
- specific technologies.

These require current review.

---

# RULE

Do not delete useful older instruction merely because it is old.

Instead classify it.

## OLD ≠ WRONG.

## OLD + CHANGED FACTS = UPDATE REQUIRED.
