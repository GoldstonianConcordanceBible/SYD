# SYD-MKT-101 — MARKET UNIVERSE

## THE STOCK MARKET ON GDEX

This course is focused on traditional financial markets available through the GDex market interface.

The purpose is to teach the learner how to move across multiple familiar market categories without requiring them to leave the learning environment.

The primary market universe is:

STOCKS

↓

INDICES

↓

COMMODITIES

↓

FOREX

↓

PRE-IPO

Crypto is intentionally outside the scope of this course.

Crypto receives separate courses later.

---

# WHY THIS MATTERS

A learner may initially arrive because they want to trade:

Apple.

Tesla.

Google.

The S&P 500.

But once they understand the same market-analysis framework, they should be able to examine additional traditional markets.

Examples include:

Gold.

Silver.

Oil.

Natural Gas.

Foreign exchange.

Pre-IPO market exposure where available.

The learner should not need a completely different educational system for every asset class.

The framework remains:

OBSERVE

↓

IDENTIFY THE INSTRUMENT

↓

FORM A THESIS

↓

DEFINE INVALIDATION

↓

IDENTIFY RISK

↓

DOCUMENT

↓

REVIEW

# MARKET CATEGORY 5 — PRE-IPO

Pre-IPO products require particularly careful instrument identification.

A product labeled with the name of a private company does not automatically mean the learner owns private-company shares.

Before discussing a Pre-IPO market, identify:

- what the product represents,
- who provides it,
- how the price is determined,
- whether settlement occurs,
- whether the product converts into equity,
- what happens if the company never completes an IPO,
- what jurisdictional or eligibility restrictions apply.

---

# PRE-IPO DOCTRINE

## COMPANY NAME ≠ PRIVATE EQUITY OWNERSHIP.

Always identify the actual instrument.
