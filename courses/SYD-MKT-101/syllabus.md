# DESCRIPTION

This case-study course teaches students how to reconstruct, analyze, and evaluate a bullish market thesis using a documented S&P 500-related case.

The emphasis is on decision quality rather than hindsight.

---

# PREREQUISITES

Recommended:

SYD-MKT-000 — Get On the Platform

No prior professional trading experience required.

---

# COURSE LEARNING OUTCOMES

By completion, learners will be able to:

1. explain what the S&P 500 represents,
2. distinguish the index from instruments used to gain exposure,
3. explain what a long thesis means,
4. construct a bounded market thesis,
5. define explicit invalidation,
6. identify material risk,
7. reconstruct a trade chronologically,
8. separate process quality from outcome quality.

---

# LEARNING ACTIVITIES

- short lessons,
- market chart review,
- instrument-identification exercise,
- historical evidence reconstruction,
- thesis card,
- invalidation exercise,
- after-action review.

---

# FINAL ASSESSMENT

Learner receives a market scenario and produces:

- instrument identification,
- thesis,
- invalidation,
- material risks,
- catalyst list,
- review schedule,
- after-action analysis.

---

# PASSING STANDARD

80% mastery.

Instrument identification and invalidation are critical criteria.

---

# MARKET RESULT POLICY

Profit does not automatically equal mastery.

Loss does not automatically equal failure.

The assessment evaluates process.

---

# AI USE

AI may assist brainstorming and explanation where allowed.

The learner must personally defend:

- instrument identification,
- thesis,
- invalidation,
- risk analysis.

---

# FINANCIAL EDUCATION BOUNDARY

No individualized investment recommendation is required to complete the course.

Learners may use:

- historical examples,
- paper trading,
- simulation,
- hypothetical cases.
