# DESCRIPTION

This course introduces equity and index markets through a case-based analysis of a historical S&P 500 long thesis.

Students learn to distinguish:

- a market,
- an index,
- a company,
- a security,
- an instrument,
- a trade.

The course emphasizes disciplined reasoning rather than retrospective storytelling.

---

# PREREQUISITE

Recommended:

SYD-MKT-000 — Financial Markets Orientation & Platform Onboarding

---

# LEARNING OUTCOMES

By completion, learners will be able to:

1. distinguish equities from indices,
2. explain what the S&P 500 represents,
3. distinguish an index from an instrument providing exposure to it,
4. record objective market observations,
5. construct a bounded thesis,
6. define explicit invalidation,
7. identify material risk,
8. reconstruct a historical market case chronologically,
9. separate process quality from outcome quality.
