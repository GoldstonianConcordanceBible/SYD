# SYD-MKT-101 LAB ENVIRONMENT

## ACADEMIC CORE

Vendor neutral.

---

# PRIMARY DEVELOPMENT LAB

GDex

---

# PURPOSE

The laboratory allows students to observe:

- equity markets,
- index markets,
- charts,
- price movement,
- available instruments,
- execution interfaces.

---

# ALTERNATIVES

An instructor may use:

- historical data,
- paper trading,
- simulation,
- another approved platform,
- institutional market systems.

---

# REQUIRED CAPABILITIES

The laboratory should support:

1. market observation,
2. instrument identification,
3. chart review,
4. thesis exercise,
5. risk discussion.
