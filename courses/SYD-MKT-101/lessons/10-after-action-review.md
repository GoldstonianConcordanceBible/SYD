# LESSON 10 — AFTER-ACTION REVIEW

Ask:

1. What did I know before the result?
2. What did I assume?
3. What evidence was strongest?
4. What evidence did I ignore?
5. Was invalidation explicit?
6. Was risk understood?
7. Did I follow the thesis?
8. What part of the outcome was skill?
9. What part was luck?
10. What would I change?

---

# TWO SCORES

## PROCESS SCORE

Was the decision process disciplined?

## OUTCOME SCORE

What happened financially or directionally?

Do not combine these into one number.
