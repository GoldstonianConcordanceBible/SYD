# WHY

The objective is not to teach a student twenty different platforms.

The objective is to teach one repeatable decision process.

OBSERVE.

IDENTIFY.

FORM A THESIS.

DEFINE INVALIDATION.

UNDERSTAND RISK.

DOCUMENT.

REVIEW.

Then apply that framework to another market.

---
