# LESSON 7 — RISK

Direction is only one part of a market decision.

---

# MARKET RISK

The market moves against the thesis.

---

# INSTRUMENT RISK

The financial product behaves differently than expected.

---

# EXECUTION RISK

Examples:

- spread,
- slippage,
- liquidity.

---

# LEVERAGE RISK

Leverage may amplify gains and losses.

---

# PLATFORM RISK

Examples:

- outages,
- restrictions,
- execution problems.

---

# EVENT RISK

Unexpected information changes market conditions.
