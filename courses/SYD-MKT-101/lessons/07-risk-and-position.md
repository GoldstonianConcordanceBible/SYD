# LESSON 7 — RISK

Knowing direction is not enough.

Potential risks include:

- market reversal,
- leverage,
- liquidation,
- gap movement,
- spread,
- slippage,
- liquidity,
- platform failure,
- oracle failure,
- contract-specific risk.

---

# POSITION SIZE

This course teaches the concept of bounded risk.

It does not require a learner to risk real capital.

---

# EXERCISE

Compare two hypothetical positions with the same bullish thesis but different:

- leverage,
- volatility,
- liquidity.

Explain why they do not carry equal risk.
