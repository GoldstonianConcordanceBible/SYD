# LESSON 6 — INVALIDATION

Invalidation answers:

> What evidence would demonstrate that the original thesis is no longer sufficiently supported?

---

# INVALIDATION CAN INVOLVE

- price,
- market structure,
- news,
- economic data,
- time,
- volatility,
- thesis-specific evidence.

---

# BAD INVALIDATION

"I'll close when I feel uncomfortable."

---

# BETTER INVALIDATION

A predefined observable event tied to the thesis.

---

# CORE DOCTRINE

## A THESIS WITHOUT INVALIDATION IS INCOMPLETE.

---

Write one bullish thesis.
