# LESSON 1 — EQUITIES + INDICES

## COMPANY

An operating organization.

Example:

Apple.

---

# STOCK

A financial interest associated with a company.

---

# INDEX

A benchmark constructed from a defined group of securities.

Example:

S&P 500.

---

# TRADE

A specific market position involving an actual instrument.

---

# IMPORTANT

Apple the company

is not identical to:

AAPL the quoted security

and neither description alone tells you:

what instrument a specific platform provides.
