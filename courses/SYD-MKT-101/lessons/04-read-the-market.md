# LESSON 5 — BUILD THE THESIS

A useful thesis answers:

## WHAT?

What do I believe?

## WHY?

What evidence supports it?

## WHEN?

What time horizon matters?

## WHAT CHANGES MY MIND?

What invalidates it?
