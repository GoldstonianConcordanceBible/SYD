# LESSON 1 — WHAT IS THE S&P 500?

The S&P 500 is a market index designed to measure the performance of a broad group of large U.S. companies.

It is useful as:

- a benchmark,
- a market reference,
- a foundation for multiple tradeable products.

---

# IMPORTANT

The index itself and the product used to gain exposure are not automatically the same thing.
