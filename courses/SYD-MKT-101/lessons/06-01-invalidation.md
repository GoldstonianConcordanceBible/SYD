# LESSON 6 — INVALIDATION

Invalidation defines the condition under which the thesis is no longer sufficiently supported.

---

# INVALIDATION MAY INVOLVE

- price,
- market structure,
- time,
- economic data,
- company information,
- volatility,
- thesis-specific evidence.

---

# WEAK INVALIDATION

"I will leave if I get nervous."

---

# STRONGER INVALIDATION

A predefined observable condition tied directly to the thesis.

---
