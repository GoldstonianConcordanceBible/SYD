# LESSON 3 — WHAT DOES LONG MEAN?

A bullish or long thesis expresses the expectation that the relevant exposure will benefit if the market moves upward.

But:

LONG SPY

LONG ES FUTURES

LONG A CALL OPTION

LONG A SYNTHETIC PERPETUAL

are not economically identical positions.

---

# WHY THIS MATTERS

The thesis may be similar.

The risk mechanics may differ dramatically.
