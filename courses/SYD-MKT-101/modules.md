# MODULES

# MODULE 0 — THE TRADITIONAL MARKET UNIVERSE

Before focusing on the S&P 500, learners examine the market categories available inside the GDex TradFi environment.

Categories include:

- Stocks
- Indices
- Commodities
- FX
- Pre-IPO

The purpose is not to master every market immediately.

The purpose is to understand that the same disciplined analytical framework can travel across multiple markets.

## MODULE 1 — WHAT IS THE S&P 500?

The index.

Its purpose.

Its components.

Why people use it as a broad U.S. equity-market reference.

---

## MODULE 2 — INDEX VS INSTRUMENT

The index is not necessarily the product being traded.

Students learn to identify:

- index,
- ETF,
- futures,
- options,
- synthetic exposure,
- perpetual exposure,
- other derivatives.

---

## MODULE 3 — WHAT DOES LONG MEAN?

A long thesis generally expresses an expectation that the value of the relevant position or exposure will increase.

The actual mechanics depend upon the instrument.

---

## MODULE 4 — READ THE MARKET

Observe:

- price,
- timeframe,
- trend,
- recent range,
- volatility,
- catalysts.

No thesis yet.

Observation comes first.

---

## MODULE 5 — BUILD THE THESIS

Structure:

WHAT I BELIEVE

WHY I BELIEVE IT

WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTS IT

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND

---

## MODULE 6 — INVALIDATION

A thesis must have a failure condition.

If there is no possible evidence that changes the thesis, it is not a useful trading thesis.

---

## MODULE 7 — RISK

Identify:

- market risk,
- instrument risk,
- leverage,
- liquidity,
- gap risk,
- technology/platform risk where applicable.

---

## MODULE 8 — CASE CHRONOLOGY

Reconstruct CASE-001 using timestamped evidence.

Do not jump directly to the outcome.

---

## MODULE 9 — CLOSE AND RESULT

What happened?

What ended the formal case?

Was it:

- target / decision close,
- invalidation,
- expiration,
- cancellation,
- another reason?

---

## MODULE 10 — AFTER-ACTION REVIEW

Evaluate:

- thesis quality,
- evidence quality,
- invalidation quality,
- execution,
- discipline,
- luck,
- repeatability.
