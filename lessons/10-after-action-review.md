# LESSON 10 — AFTER-ACTION REVIEW

Ask:

1. What did I actually know?
2. What did I assume?
3. What evidence was strongest?
4. What evidence did I ignore?
5. Was the thesis specific?
6. Was invalidation explicit?
7. Were risks understood?
8. Was the process followed?
9. What part was skill?
10. What part may have been luck?
11. What should change next time?
