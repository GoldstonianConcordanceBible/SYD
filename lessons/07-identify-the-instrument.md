# LESSON 7 — WHAT ARE YOU ACTUALLY TRADING?

## The Most Important Onboarding Question

A symbol is not enough.

If a screen says:

S&P 500

you must ask:

> What is the contract or instrument behind this interface?

---

# POSSIBILITIES

It may represent:

- an ETF,
- index derivative,
- perpetual,
- CFD-like product,
- synthetic market,
- tokenized representation,
- another instrument.

---

# QUESTIONS TO ASK

1. What is the instrument called?
2. Who issues or operates it?
3. Is there expiration?
4. Is leverage involved?
5. What is the collateral?
6. How is price determined?
7. Is an oracle used?
8. What happens during liquidation?
9. Where is the product legally available?

---

## THE SYMBOL DOES NOT TELL YOU THE LEGAL INSTRUMENT.
