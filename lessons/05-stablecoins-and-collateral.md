# LESSON 5 — STABLECOINS AND COLLATERAL

## Stablecoins

Stablecoins are digital assets designed to track the value of another asset, commonly a fiat currency.

They are not risk-free.

Potential risks include:

- issuer risk,
- reserve risk,
- depeg risk,
- smart-contract risk,
- platform risk.

---

# COLLATERAL

Some platforms require collateral before a position can be opened.

Collateral is not the same thing as the market being traded.

---

# EXAMPLE

A learner may deposit a stablecoin as collateral while trading synthetic S&P 500 exposure.

The collateral and the market instrument are different objects.
