# LESSON 8 — FIRST MARKET OBSERVATION

No live-money trade is required.

Observe one market.

Record:

- asset,
- instrument,
- current price,
- timeframe,
- direction,
- spread,
- volatility,
- available risk disclosures.

---

# DO NOT FORM A TRADE JUST TO COMPLETE THE COURSE

Observation is enough.

## NO TRADE IS A VALID DECISION.
