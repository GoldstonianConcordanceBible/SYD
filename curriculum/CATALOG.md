# SYD MASTER COURSE CATALOG

**Division:** SydTek Scholars  
**Version:** v0.1.0  
**Status:** ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

# PURPOSE

This catalog is the canonical inventory of planned, active, pilot, live, paused, and retired SYD learning objects.

A listing here does not mean the course is finished.

The catalog is intentionally ahead of production.

Its purpose is to convert ideas into identifiable future learning products.

---

# STATUS KEY

IDEA — concept captured.

DESIGN — outcomes and structure being written.

ALPHA — internal prototype.

PILOT — limited learner testing.

LIVE — available to learners.

REVIEW — undergoing significant review.

PAUSED — intentionally stopped.

RETIRED — no longer sold or taught.

ARCHIVED — preserved as historical evidence.
